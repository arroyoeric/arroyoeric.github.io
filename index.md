## Favorite CPSC 120L Labs

* Lab 4

Lab 4 consisted of two parts, the first part was converting units, such as ML to ounces; 
the second part was the one I liked better. In the second part we created a short game called “hilo” 
which was basically just guessing a secret number. I see myself as a “gamer” so creating a game on a 
small scale like this was cool to do and made me have an interest in game development. In the coding, 
we practiced using if statements, as well as operators such as != and ==.

* Lab 6

Lab 6 revolved around making sandwiches and the game blackjack. Although I do enjoy dabbling 
in sandwiches, I like to go to the casinos and gamble a little more. When I turned 21 I had 
gone to the casino with my dad and spent some time on the blackjack table, as well as roulette. 
It was fun to code and create a blackjack game on a smaller scale, seeing how the roots of the 
game work. In this lab we practiced if statements more, with else conditions, as all as more operators, 
and some logic too.

* Lab 7

Lab 7 made us convert the rules provided on a parking sign into code. I liked this lab because it 
made us really think and use logic more than anything. Each sign had different days and times permitted 
and it all felt like one big mystery to solve. This lab also had us practice more with if statements with 
else conditions. Labs like this helped gain more confidence in coding, especially focusing on one particular 
aspect at a time such as if statements.
